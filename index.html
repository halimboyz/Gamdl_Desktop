Everything the command line made you do by hand

Search or paste a link

Search Apple Music inside the app, or paste an album, playlist, artist or song link. Pick your storefront country so results match your region.

Queue it and walk away

Run up to 8 downloads at once. When the queue ends, a notification tells you how many worked and how many failed, and one click retries the failed ones.

Albums arrive as tracks

Albums split into separate files automatically, with cover art attached. Both are settings you can turn off.

Hear it before you keep it

Preview full songs with gapless playback and synced lyrics. Previews play from a temporary folder and are deleted afterwards.

From install to first download in three steps

  1. Step 1

    Set up once

    On first launch GAMDL checks for Python, gamdl, mpv and WebView2. If anything is missing, it asks and then installs it for you.

  2. Step 2

    Sign in to Apple Music

    Add your cookies file in Settings, or complete the one time sign in window that gamdl opens.

  3. Step 3

    Search, queue, download

    Pick songs, albums or playlists and press download. Files land in the output folder you set in Settings.

Questions before you download

Do I need an Apple Music subscription?

Yes. GAMDL signs in with your own Apple Music session through gamdl, so it can only fetch what your subscription can play.

What gets installed on my PC?

Python 3.10 or newer, gamdl 4, mpv for full song previews, and the Microsoft Edge WebView2 runtime. GAMDL shows you the list and waits for a yes before it downloads anything.

Does it run on Mac or Linux?

No. GAMDL is a Windows app built with AutoHotkey v2 and WebView2.

Where do my files and settings go?

Music goes to the output folder you set in Settings. History, favorites and settings are saved in a .gamdl folder inside your Windows user profile.

Can I download several albums at once?

Yes. Run between 1 and 8 downloads in parallel, with 4 as the default. Albums can be split into separate tracks as they download.

Is GAMDL affiliated with Apple?

No. It is an independent app made by HLIM. Use it for your own listening, and follow the terms of your subscription and the copyright rules where you live.
